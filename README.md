**Task 10 - Simple KPI Tracking Sheet
Internship task for Veda Technology (Data Analytics track, Level 1, Day 10).**

**About**
This task was about creating a dynamic one-page KPI summary that recalculates automatically when new rows are added to the sales data. The goal was to practice translating raw transaction data into key metrics using Excel formulas instead of manual calculations.

**Dataset**
Used the Sample Superstore dataset (100 rows, 9 columns).

**Tools Used**
- Microsoft Excel
**
What I Did**
- Converted raw data into an Excel Table (SalesTable) so formulas auto-update when new data is added
- Calculated 5 main KPIs using formulas: Total Revenue, Total Units Sold, Total Orders, Average Order Value, and Top Product
- Used SUMIFS to break down performance by product category (Furniture, Technology, Office Supplies)
- Evaluated what factors were actually driving the revenue and average order numbers

**What I Found**
- Furniture accounted for only 30% of orders but generated 57.3% of total revenue because of high unit prices.
- Office Supplies had almost as many orders (29%) but contributed only 2.0% of total revenue since items are cheap.
- Technology was balanced with 41% of orders and 40.6% of revenue.
- The top product came out as Office Chair because it was calculated on total sales value, not order count.
- Main takeaway: A single average order value can look good while hiding very different behaviors underneath. Always break metrics down by category to see what's actually driving the numbers.
